# PDF Display - Before and After

## Pre-Wedding 1-Day (₹20,000 base) with Service Increases

### BEFORE (Incorrect)
```
The Pre-Wedding
Item                 Unit Price    Qty    Total
Candid Photo          Rs. 8,000      2    Rs. 16,000  ❌
Candid Video          Rs. 10,000     3    Rs. 30,000  ❌
Drone                 Rs. 8,000      1    Rs. 8,000   ❌

Event Total: Rs. 54,000
```
**Problem:** Shows each service being charged individually, as if they're not part of the package.

### AFTER (Correct)
```
The Pre-Wedding
Item                 Unit Price    Qty    Total
Candid Photo          Rs. 8,000      2      -       ✅
Candid Video          Rs. 10,000     3      -       ✅
Drone                 Rs. 8,000      1      -       ✅

Extra Service Charges:
+ Candid Photo: (1) × Rs. 8,000           = Rs. 8,000
+ Candid Video: (2) × Rs. 10,000          = Rs. 20,000

Event Total: Rs. 48,000  ✅
```
**Correct:** Shows that base 1× of each is included (not charged), extra quantities are clearly shown separately.

## Summary of Changes

| Aspect | Before | After |
|--------|--------|-------|
| Pre/Post-Wedding qty=1 | Shows price (confusing) | Shows "-" (included in package) |
| Pre/Post-Wedding qty>1 | Shows full price | Shows "-" + extra charges section |
| Extra charges display | Hidden | Clearly shown with breakdown |
| Other events | Normal | Unchanged |
| Clarity | Confusing pricing | Clear package vs. extra logic |

## Key Benefits
✅ **Clear Package Logic**: Users understand what's included vs. what's extra  
✅ **Accurate Pricing**: No confusion about double-charging or package inclusions  
✅ **Professional Look**: Separate extra charges section is well-formatted  
✅ **Consistent**: Both download and share functions use same format
